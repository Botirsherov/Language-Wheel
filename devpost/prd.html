Product requirements · based on approved scope

Word Wheel

A web app for people who want a simple, hands-on way to practice vocabulary with their own words or an essential-word set.

The learner's journey

What each surface does

Main page / wheel

Starts with an empty wheel and a central add sign; titled example blocks appear beneath it. Choosing an example loads 15–20 words. A spin button is available once a set is loaded.

Add words page

Separate page for one-at-a-time entry. Word and translation are required; color is optional. Added entries appear below the fields. Start requires at least two entries.

Practice card

Spin selects one word. The learner tries to recall it, taps the card to reveal the translation, then chooses “I got it” or “Not yet.”

Round complete

After every word has left the current round, the learner can restore the words and restart or finish the round.

Behavior · choosing a set and adding words
  • Offer English–Spanish, English–French, and English–German essential-word examples, each with 15–20 words.
  • Example choice loads its words onto the wheel; custom words have no language selection.
  • Require both word and translation before adding an entry. Show added words below the form.
  • Warn and do not start when fewer than two custom words are present.
Behavior · spin, reveal, and self-assess
  • Show the selected word on a card; tapping flips it to reveal its translation.
  • “I got it” removes that word for this round; “Not yet” keeps it for another turn.
  • When all words have left the wheel, offer restart or finish. Restart restores the words.
Acceptance checks · observable proof
  • First opening shows an empty wheel, central add sign, and titled example blocks.
  • Choosing each ready-made set loads 15–20 words and enables spinning.
  • The add sign opens the separate entry page; added complete pairs appear below the form.
  • An incomplete pair is not added and produces a warning. Starting with fewer than two words warns and does not begin practice.
  • Spinning shows a word; tapping reveals its translation. “I got it” removes it for the round; “Not yet” keeps it available.
  • When all words have left the round, restart restores them and finish ends the round.
Look and feel

Use a clear, cheerful style that makes the colorful wheel prominent and controls easy to understand. The learner delegated visual choices to the assistant and did not specify a font, palette, or reference.

Out of scope and later

Accounts and saved progress are deferred to a later version. The first version does not automatically grade spoken or typed translations; learners self-assess.

Open for build time

The exact words in the three ready-made sets can be chosen during the build, as requested.

Canonical requirements are in prd.md. This visual companion is designed to work offline.
