Project scope · proof of concept

Word Wheel

A web app where learners practice vocabulary with a colorful spinning wheel and a flip-to-reveal translation card.

From learner to proof

The idea

Distinctive kernel

Words marked “I got it” leave the current round. “Not yet” words stay; when the round ends, words return for another practice round.

Who it helps

People who have trouble learning and remembering vocabulary, including learners practicing English–Spanish, English–French, or English–German.

Why it matters

The learner sees many people struggle to learn words and wants to make a web app to help them practice.

What working looks like

Someone opens a page with a large wheel and example blocks, chooses words or adds their own, spins to get a word, flips its card to reveal the translation, and marks whether they remembered it. Correct words leave the current round while missed words remain; when the round is over, the words return so they can practice again.

Now · the proof of concept
  • One page with a large wheel, an add sign, and titled essential-word examples.
  • Add words and translations; optionally choose colors for words.
  • Include a few ready-made words for English–Spanish, English–French, and English–German.
  • Spin, reveal the translation by flipping a card, self-mark recall, and restart a round.
Later · beyond the first version

Accounts that save progress between visits.
