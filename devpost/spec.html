Technical blueprint · no backend needed

Word Wheel

A small HTML, CSS, and JavaScript app that runs in your browser. Your words travel between its pages in temporary storage for the current browser tab.

How the parts connect

One word, through the app

1 · Choose or enter

Example data or the add form supplies word and translation pairs.

2 · Keep in this tab

JavaScript puts the current set and round into the browser's temporary notebook, sessionStorage.

3 · Practice and update

The wheel picks a word; the card reveals its translation; a recall choice updates the current round.

What is the app made of?
  • index.html — main page, examples, wheel, card, and round controls.
  • add-words.html — one-at-a-time custom word entry and list.
  • app.js — page interactions, validation, spin and recall behavior, tab storage.
  • word-sets.js — the three built-in example lists.
  • styles.css — colorful wheel, responsive layout, and card animation.
Where the words live

Custom words and the active round are kept in sessionStorage, which acts like a short-term notebook for this browser tab. They stay available when navigating between the two pages or refreshing in this tab, but are cleared when the tab closes. There is no account, database, or promise of saving progress for another visit.

Run it and record the demo
  1. From the project folder, run py -m http.server 8000 (or python -m http.server 8000).
  2. Open http://localhost:8000/ in a browser.
  3. Choose an example or add at least two custom words, then show spin, card reveal, both recall outcomes, and restart/finish.

No packages, API keys, or deployment are required. The submission still needs a short demo video and a public GitHub repository.

Tradeoffs and open build detail

This keeps the proof of concept local and avoids a backend; closing the tab clears temporary words. Exact example vocabulary can be selected during the build. Use a clear, cheerful appearance and keep the wheel prominent.
